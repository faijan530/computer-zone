Airflow, engineered like a wind tunnel.

No fans doesn't mean no cooling — it means the heat pipe network is doing all the work.

  • Vapor chamber: spreads heat across the full chassis instead of one hot spot.
  • Dual heat pipes: carry warmth away from the CPU and GPU simultaneously.
  • Passive exhaust: heat exits through the hinge, with no moving parts to wear out.
